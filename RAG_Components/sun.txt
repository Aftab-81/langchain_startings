# The Sun

The golden Sun begins the day,
And paints the clouds in bright array.
It warms the earth with gentle light,
And fills the world with colors bright.

It wakes the flowers, birds, and trees,
And dances softly with the breeze.
It shines above the hills so high,
Like a golden lamp across the sky.

When evening comes, it slowly goes,
And leaves the sky in sunset glows.
Then night arrives, calm and deep—
While the shining Sun goes off to sleep.
